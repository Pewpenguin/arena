To determine which is larger between the fractions $ \frac{3}{4} $ and $ \frac{2}{3} $, we can use several methods:

---

### 1. **Decimal Conversion**  
- $ \frac{3}{4} = 0.75 $  
- $ \frac{2}{3} = 0.\overline{6} $ (a repeating decimal)  

Comparing the two:  
- $ 0.75 > 0.666\ldots $, so $ \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3} $.

---

### 2. **Common Denominator**  
- The least common denominator (LCD) of 4 and 3 is 12.  
- Convert both fractions to have denominator 12:  
  - $ \frac{3}{4} = \frac{9}{12} $  
  - $ \frac{2}{3} = \frac{8}{12} $  

Comparing the numerators:  
- $ 9 > 8 $, so $ \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3} $.

---

### 3. **Cross-Multiplication**  
To compare $ \frac{a}{b} $ and $ \frac{c}{d} $, compare $ a \cdot d $ and $ c \cdot b $:  
- $ 3 \cdot 3 = 9 $  
- $ 2 \cdot 4 = 8 $  

Since $ 9 > 8 $, $ \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3} $.

---

### 4. **Percentage Conversion**  
- $ \frac{3}{4} = 75\% $  
- $ \frac{2}{3} \approx 66.67\% $  

Since $ 75\% > 66.67\% $, $ \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3} $.

---

### 5. **Visual/Conceptual Interpretation**  
Imagine two pizzas of the same size:  
- One cut into 4 equal slices, taking 3 (each slice is 25% of the pizza).  
- Another cut into 3 equal slices, taking 2 (each slice is ~33.33% of the pizza).  

- 3 slices of 25% = 75% of the pizza  
- 2 slices of 33.33% = 66.66% of the pizza  

So, 75% > 66.66%, confirming $ \frac{3}{4} > \frac{2}{3} $.
